The administrator oversees all operations in the wedding planning system, managing clients, bookings, budget, and more. They set up wedding packages to kickstart the process and manage features within these packages.
Admin uploads images and descriptions to showcase the available services for potential clients. They also handle user accounts, including managers and admin.
Admin publishes blogs and events for clients to view, providing inspiration for their own weddings.
Clients can book packages themselves, with admin verifying and confirming bookings. 
Budget management is crucial, with admin overseeing payments and expenses for each client.
They maintain a database of VIP guests, ensuring they receive personalized attention and assistance during the event. 
Task reminders are set within each client’s profile to ensure nothing is overlooked, ensuring that every aspect of the wedding is carefully planned and executed. 
The system serves as both a client engagement platform and a portfolio for the wedding planning company.
Overall, the wedding planning system simplifies the entire process, allowing clients to focus on enjoying their special day while leaving the logistics to the experts.
